### Colourways

`colourways-dial.png` shows the approved text-on-background pairings, from "Proven" to "Audacious":

- **Proven:** Dark Blue on White or Limestone; White or Limestone on Dark Blue.
- **Middle ground:** Purple on White; White on Purple; White on Bright Blue; Bright Blue on White.
- **Audacious:** Limestone on Bright Blue; Purple on Dark Blue; Purple on Limestone; Dark Blue on Purple.

Contrast (WCAG): Dark Blue on White 17:1, White on Bright Blue 6.2:1, Purple and Dark Blue together 4.5:1. Purple and White together are only 3.8:1, which is enough for large text but not for body-size text.

## Typography

| Role | Brand font | System fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | National 2 Condensed Bold (700) | Arial Bold |
| Sub headline / pull quote | National 2 Condensed Medium | Arial Bold |
| Body | Roboto Serif Regular (300, 400, 400 italic, 600 available) | Georgia Regular |

- National 2 Condensed is listed as a desktop font. Roboto Serif is the web font (Google Fonts).
- Use system fonts only when the brand fonts can't be used (e.g. eDMs, PPTs).
- Headlines are sentence case. All caps is only for the "Flexing the grid" graphic device.
- This site uses Barlow Condensed (Google Fonts) as a web stand-in for National 2 Condensed.
